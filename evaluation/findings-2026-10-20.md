## Findings that justify follow-up

1. Trace CQ03's explicit Weimar track MBID and MusicBrainz recording/version
   against their matching snapshots before reporting verified identity.
2. Keep CTO related-person answers role-unspecified. CQ05/CQ06 demonstrate
   query scope; source role/identity checking remains pending. Live RISM output
   does not validate the upstream P50-to-P86 mapping.
3. Test candidate MusicBrainz/APSearch representations on staging with exact
   schema and graph IRIs. Empty production responses cannot evaluate undeployed
   code, and CQ10's failure remains unresolved.
4. Build complete reconciliation frames from source/intermediate exports,
   freeze quotas/seeds and perform the proposed 60 reviews including unlinked
   units. CQ14's ordered results are not that sample.
5. Populate snapshot-specific licences, dates, checksums and dated counts;
   trace selected source occurrences with the fidelity template.
